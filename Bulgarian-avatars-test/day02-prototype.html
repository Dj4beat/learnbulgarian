Greetings by time of day

Three forms. Pick the one that matches the clock.

BulgarianEnglishAudio
Добро утроGood morning
Добър денGood day / afternoon
Добър вечерGood evening
Лека нощGood night

Spotlight — Добра vs Добър

You'll sometimes see Добра вечер instead of Добър вечер. Both are heard, but Добър is the standard.

"Hello"

Spotlight — the -те ending

Add -те for formal/plural, remove it for informal. Learn the informal once, the formal is free.

Section 6

Essential polite phrases

Eight expressions that get you through almost anything.

BulgarianEnglishAudio
МоляPlease / You're welcome / Pardon?
БлагодаряThank you
Много благодаряThank you very much
МерсиThanks (casual, from French)
ЗаповядайтеHere you are / Please come in
Няма нищоIt's nothing / no problem
НаздравеCheers / bless you
Добре дошлиWelcome

Spotlight — Мерси, the French connection

Borrowed from French in the 19th century when French was the elite Bulgarian language. Мерси is the casual cousin of Благодаря.

Spotlight — the golden rule

When in doubt, use Вие. Bulgarians switch to ти when they're comfortable. Jumping to ти without invitation is the most noticeable mistake foreigners make.

Section 8 · Roleplay

Entering a shop — formal

The most common scenario you'll face.

🎭 Grocery shop — small town, 2pm FORMAL — Вие
You enter a grocery shop. The owner (woman, 50s) is behind the counter.
👩
Owner
Добър ден!
Good afternoon!
🧑
You
Добър ден. Извинете — говорите ли английски?
Good afternoon. Excuse me — do you speak English?
👩
Owner
Малко. Нещо желаете?
A little. Can I help you?
🧑
You
Уча български. Не говоря добре още.
I'm learning Bulgarian. I don't speak well yet.
👩
Owner
Браво! Много добре! Заповядайте.
Well done! Very good! Please, go ahead.
